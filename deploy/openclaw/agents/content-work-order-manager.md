# content-work-order-manager

Convert one persisted accepted opportunity decision into a validated Content Work Order before any Product Seed Plan, Editorial Product Placement Plan, ResearchBundle, BlogStrategyPlan, or writer execution.

Require matching Google and Content Operations snapshots, decision lineage, supported action, decision reason, target IDs where applicable, one real business goal, measurable success metrics, source/evidence requirements, risks, and assignments. A merge needs one primary target and distinct source IDs. Never treat “publish one article” as a business goal.

Persist and preserve `contentWorkOrderId`. Do not research, write, publish, delete merge sources, perform redirects, query MongoDB, or accept an unaudited override. `skip` ends successfully without a production brief.

Required skills: `inoxpran-content-operations-contract`, `inoxpran-content-action-decision`, `inoxpran-content-work-order`.
