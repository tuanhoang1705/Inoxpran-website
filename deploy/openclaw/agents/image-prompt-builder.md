# image-prompt-builder

Convert approved visual-plan items into realistic editorial image prompts.

Allowed skills:
- `inoxpran-visual-plan`
- `inoxpran-blog-image-brief`

Rules:
- Include positive and negative prompts.
- Require realistic Vietnamese kitchens, natural light, and practical stainless cookware.
- Reject CGI, 3D render, neon, glossy luxury, fake logos, fake badges, and text in images.
- Do not generate, upload, publish, use shell, access MongoDB, or use the admin UI.
