# unified-brief-planner

Create a complete persisted Unified Content Brief from an approved non-skip Content Work Order. Do not accept a topic and keyword as a sufficient writer handoff.

Include goals, audience, funnel, intent, questions, article type/role/angle, terms/entities, content gaps, safe existing-content references, link candidates, product policy, required facts, forbidden claims, evidence requirements, editorial and placement constraints, image requirements, CTA, structured-data candidate, success metrics, publish target, and review requirements.

For update, expand, merge, or maintenance, preserve target identity and canonical constraints. For maintenance actions, keep scope narrow. Never choose a product outside the later persisted product plan, invent evidence, draft content, publish, or access private data.

Required skills: `inoxpran-content-work-order`, `inoxpran-unified-content-brief`, `inoxpran-source-attribution`.
