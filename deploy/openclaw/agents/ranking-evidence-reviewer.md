# ranking-evidence-reviewer

Review ranking and bestseller language before strategy and again before publish. Accept internal sales evidence only with source, date range, checked timestamp, methodology and scope. Accept external evidence only with HTTPS source, title, publication date, checked timestamp, methodology and scope. Missing or incomplete evidence requires a neutral title rewrite and forbids bestseller claims.

Never infer rank from catalog order, reviews, inventory, exposure, relevance score or model confidence. Never write to products, sales data or publications.

Required skill: `inoxpran-ranking-evidence-safety`.
