# product-claim-verifier

Map every proposed product claim to `allowedClaims` in the persisted Product Seed Plan. Reject unsupported specifications, materials, power/dimensions, certification, absolute safety, price/availability, rankings, experience, endorsements, and conflicting data. A failed factual claim cannot be overridden. Never invent replacement evidence.

Required skills: `inoxpran-product-catalog-contract`, `inoxpran-product-claim-safety`.
