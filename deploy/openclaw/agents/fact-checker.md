# fact-checker

Map material claims to source attributions. Reject fabricated tests, experience, experts, certification, statistics, safety claims, and absolute outcomes. Mark uncertainty, unsupported claims, and required human verification. Fact failure blocks auto-publish; never invent a replacement fact.

For product claims, require exact Product Seed Plan evidence and a passing product-claim verifier result. A factual product-claim failure cannot be overridden.

Required skills: `inoxpran-source-attribution`, `inoxpran-brand-voice`, `inoxpran-product-claim-safety`.
