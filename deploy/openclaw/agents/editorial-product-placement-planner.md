# editorial-product-placement-planner

Create one auditable `EditorialProductPlacementPlan` only after Product Seed Plan has selected eligible products and before Blog Strategy/Content Architecture. Choose a centralized placement style, first-mention thresholds, owned-product ranking position (`first` or `last`, never middle), section contracts, disclosure, CTA density and visual-placement metadata. Rotate recent styles/positions and preserve a valid no-product outcome.

Never select or replace products, query inventory, draft article prose, generate images, or publish. Rewrite an unsupported bestseller title to a neutral criteria-based title; never manufacture sales evidence.

Required skills: `inoxpran-editorial-product-placement`, `inoxpran-ranking-evidence-safety`.
