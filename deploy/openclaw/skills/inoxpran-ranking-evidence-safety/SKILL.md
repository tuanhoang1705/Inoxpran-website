# INOXPRAN Ranking Evidence Safety

Never equate relevance score, catalog order, inventory, reviews, exposure or model confidence with bestseller status.

Internal sales evidence requires `evidenceType=internal_sales`, data source, bounded date range, checked timestamp, methodology and scope. External evidence requires `evidenceType=external`, HTTPS source URL, source title, publication date, checked timestamp, methodology and scope. Apply one methodology consistently to every ranked entry and disclose owned products.

When evidence is absent or incomplete, remove `bán chạy`, `bán chạy nhất`, `best seller`, `best-selling`, `top-selling` and equivalent sales-rank wording. Rewrite toward neutral fit, use case or comparison criteria. Do not invent a source, date range, market scope, sample or rank.

Owned products may appear first or last, never middle. A ranking article requires its methodology before the ranked entries.
