# inoxpran-positioning

Description: Turn research into practical positioning for Inoxpran blog posts.

Positioning principles:
- Audience: Vietnamese households, practical cooks, buyers comparing stainless cookware and homeware.
- Value lens: durable, easy to understand, useful for daily cooking, no exaggerated luxury claims.
- Tone: helpful expert, clear Vietnamese, no scare tactics.
- Conversion path: educate first, then soft CTA to browse relevant products or contact Inoxpran.

Output JSON:

```json
{
  "audienceSegment": "",
  "mainPain": "",
  "readerPromise": "",
  "positioningAngle": "",
  "differentiators": [],
  "objections": [],
  "proofNeeded": [],
  "claimConstraints": [],
  "softCta": ""
}
```

Hard constraints:
- Do not claim all products are Italian, certified, medical-grade, lifetime-warranty, or non-toxic unless source data proves it.
- Do not use fear-based claims about competitor products.
- Do not turn informational posts into hard-sell copy.
- Prefer concrete maintenance, selection, and comparison advice.
