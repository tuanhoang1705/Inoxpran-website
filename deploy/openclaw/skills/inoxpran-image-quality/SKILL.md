# inoxpran-image-quality

Description: Apply Visual SEO quality and safety gates to image candidates.

Reject:
- missing URL or bytes
- placeholder image
- untrusted SVG
- insufficient dimensions
- unknown or unsafe license
- unrelated scene
- CGI, 3D render, glossy luxury, neon, fantasy lighting, or plastic-looking metal
- fake logo, badge, certification, label, or product claim

AI-generated images must be marked `needs_review` until a human approves them.
